# Privacy Policy for ASCII Garden

Last updated: November 2026

ASCII Garden is an open-source, privacy-first New Tab browser extension.

### 1. Data Collection & Privacy
ASCII Garden does **NOT** collect, track, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing history, or search queries. All features run 100% locally on your computer.

### 2. Permissions Used
- **`bookmarks`**: Used strictly to read and display your bookmarks in the customizable top bookmarks navigation bar.
- **`favicon`**: Used solely to display website icons beside your bookmark titles.
- **`search`**: Used exclusively to submit search queries to your browser's default search engine when you press Enter in the search composer.
- **`storage`**: Used solely to remember your local preferences (selected animal, clock format, custom name, and FPS limits) on your device.

### 3. Network Calls
The extension makes no background network requests, contains no third-party telemetry or analytics scripts, and displays no advertisements.

### 4. Open Source
The full source code of ASCII Garden is open and auditable at [github.com/omarekaansh-a11y/ascii-garden](https://github.com/omarekaansh-a11y/ascii-garden).
